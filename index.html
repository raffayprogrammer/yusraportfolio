AI Voice Sales Agent

Voice AI

An AI voice sales rep that answers advertising leads for a residential cleaning company — runs the sales script, checks whether the address is in service area, quotes the job and books it into the company's own booking system, on the phone and through the website.

Five tools sit behind the conversation. Coverage is a straight-line radius from the depot zip, with 20 and 33 mile bands deciding whether she quotes, captures a lead or declines. About

The short version

I'm an AI automation engineer. My work sits where n8n, third-party APIs, AI models and cloud platforms meet — mapping how disconnected systems actually depend on each other, then wiring them together so the process runs without anyone supervising it.

Most of what I build is applied rather than experimental: OCR and document processing, CRM workflows, voice agents, scheduling, content pipelines, reporting. Real business operations, with testing protocols behind them, because the failure mode of an unattended system is that nobody notices it stopped.

The part I find most interesting is the translation step — taking a problem someone has described loosely, working out what the system actually has to guarantee, and designing around the places where an API will quietly return the wrong thing rather than an error.
